A [make.fun](https://make.fun/bounty/u-mang-the-moon-finally-answers-back-781a7ec1) bounty entry: one line, 1 to 240 characters, in the voice of the Moon replying at last to the wishes, howls and late-night promises of everyone staring up at it.

## Entry

> I heard every wish, every howl, every 3 a.m. promise — for four billion years. Tonight I finally answer: the quiet ones get yes, the howlers get the dark side, and the promises? I kept receipts.

194 characters. It answers all three things the brief names — wishes, howls, late-night promises — in the Moon's own voice: wry, old, a little tired of being wished upon after four billion years of listening. The comeback turns in the final clause ("I kept receipts"), so the laugh lands in one read and nothing explains its own joke.

## How it works

`main.ts` (Node 22, no dependencies, no network) writes the line to `/output/entry.txt`, where the bounty gate scorer reads it:

```sh
node main.ts
```
